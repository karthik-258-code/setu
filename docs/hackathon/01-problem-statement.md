PROBLEM BACKGROUND

# WHY ONE PACE FAILS

## The gap grows quietly

In most classrooms, the whole class moves through the syllabus at one speed. Students who already understand a topic wait, while students who missed an earlier idea try to build on a gap.

Tests usually come at the end of a chapter, so by the time a gap shows up, the class has already moved on. Teachers want to help every student, but in a full classroom it is hard to see who needs what.

# STUDENTS AGED 11 TO 16

## What students in Classes 6 to 10 face

### Concepts build up

A basic missed in Class 6 or 7 makes the topics built on it harder later.

### Rising pressure

Closer to Class 10 and board exams, there is less time to fix old gaps.

### Many languages

Many students learn in a language different from the one spoken at home.

### Outside school

Tuition, coaching and apps add hours of study, but not always more understanding.

### Uneven access

Some students have their own device and fast internet. Many share a family phone or have limited data, and some have neither.

# THE INDIAN CONTEXT

## Built for India

Most adaptive learning apps today are built for other markets. Their algorithms aren't designed for Indian classrooms: our school boards and syllabi, large class sizes, many languages, and students with very different access to devices and internet.

# THE ROLE OF THE TEACHER

## Empower the teacher

School teachers are increasingly being sidelined by coaching centres and AI tools. Your solution should put teachers back at the centre, helping them understand each student, personalise their teaching and guide learning, instead of replacing them.

# YOUR CHALLENGE

## Make learning adapt to every student

Design a solution that adapts learning to each student in Classes 6 to 10. It should understand where a student stands and change what they learn next, how they learn it, or how fast they go.

# THREE PARTS OF STUDYING

## Learn, Solve, Test

Adaptive learning is not just about practice questions. Studying has three parts: learning a concept, solving problems and testing what you know. Your solution should adapt across all three.

### Learn — 01

Learning a concept

### Solve — 02

Solving problems

### Test — 03

# QUALITIES OF A STRONG SOLUTION

## What we're looking for

### Understands where each student stands

It finds out what a student knows and where they are stuck.

### Adapts learning, solving and testing

It changes what comes next, how and how fast, in all three parts.

### Fits Indian classrooms

Boards, languages, class sizes, and real device and internet access.

### Empowers teachers

It helps teachers understand and guide each student, not replace them.

### Practical and usable

It is simple and engaging enough for students aged 11 to 16 to actually use.
