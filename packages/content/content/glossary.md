# Telugu glossary

Terms used in Setu content (concept titles, explanations, questions, misconception labels and reason templates). Telugu follows the usage of Telangana SCERT mathematics textbooks. Every term below is a first draft and needs review by a Telugu-medium mathematics teacher before pilot; when a term changes here, change it everywhere in `content/` in the same commit.

Mathematical expressions (`3x + 5 = 11`, `2/7`) are written the same in both languages; only the words around them are translated.
